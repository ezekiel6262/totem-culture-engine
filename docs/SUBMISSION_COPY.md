# Totem submission copy

## One-line pitch

Totem makes a memecoin playable and an NFT community economically programmable through Culture Pairs, living NFT accounts, autonomous Arena economies, and DAO-governed social money.

## Project description

Totem is a culture launch and coordination engine on NEAR. A community can launch a Token, NFT collection, or Twin Culture Pair, then bind it to one rulebook, treasury, and Arena history. Culture Accounts carry identity, inventory, credentials, access, and evolving game state. Arena turns contribution into quests, player-owned assets, and deterministic seasons. Verified work becomes a capped Sputnik DAO transfer or bounty proposal that the community manages directly or through Trezu. Totem never holds the treasury and autonomous Culture Agents cannot bypass DAO approval.

## Track fit

- Memecoins With Mechanics: token communities gain rituals, quests, liquidity missions, rule-bound budgets, and culture-specific mechanics.
- NFTs That Do Something: Culture Accounts carry inventory, credentials, membership, permissions, Arena history, and evolution state.
- Games With Real Economies: autonomous season derivation, bounded wallet agents, and a player-owned asset market.
- Social Money: Sputnik and Trezu shared budgets, programmable contribution rewards, reputation-aware access, collective approvals, and public receipts.

## Three-minute demo script

1. Forge: choose Token, NFT, or Twin and show that each path asks only for its own launch parameters.
2. Culture Pair: show shared DNA, the Culture Account, and transparent fee routes.
3. Arena: enter a venue and show how proofs change identity, inventory, and the next season.
4. Economy Hall: show player-owned listings and Culture Agent spending limits.
5. Social Treasury: prepare a bounded Sputnik or Trezu proposal. Show the policy bond, proposal hash, campaign cap, and `DAO APPROVAL REQUIRED` status.
6. Close with the testnet transaction receipt and public repository.
