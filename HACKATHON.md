# NEAR HACKATHON release rules

Source of truth: https://nearhackathon.com/HACKATHON.md

Re-read the live source before submission because dates and rules can change.

## Current requirements

- Development and submission deadline: 2026-11-14 23:59 UTC.
- Repository must be public and reproducible.
- Add a license and disclose templates, libraries, AI assistance, and any pre-hackathon baseline.
- The human publishes the X demo post with `@NEARProtocol` and `#NEARHackathon` plus video or screenshots.
- Submit the public repository and public demo-post links once only.
- Never publish, force-push, post, spend funds, deploy to mainnet, or send the submission without explicit human confirmation.
- Testnet-only projects are eligible for every track.
- Judges and automated screeners must be able to follow the README as “do X, see Y” steps.

## Totem tracks

- `nfts`: Culture Accounts own behavior, inventory, credentials, memberships, access rights, royalties, and progression.
- `memecoins`: Token communities gain rule-bound treasury, rituals, challenges, bagwork, and Arena mechanics.
- `games`: Culture Agents, player-owned markets, autonomous season rules, and the Arena game loop.
- `social`: Shared budgets, programmable rewards, collective ownership, and reputation-based access.
- `open`: reciprocal Token/NFT economies and culture-owned game systems.

## Submission gate

Do not submit until every item in `SUBMISSION.md` is complete and the human has confirmed the exact final payload.
